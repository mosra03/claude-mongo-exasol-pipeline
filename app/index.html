2025 Stack Overflow Developer Survey — Live Insights

49,191 respondents · data live from Exasol

Fully remote developers earn 67% more than in-person workers ($116K vs $70K) — the largest single compensation driver in the survey.

Developers who refuse AI tools earn $110K on average — 8.7% more than daily AI users at $101K, inverting the assumption that AI boosts earnings.

Developers who see no AI job threat earn $105K vs $92K for those unsure — a $13K confidence premium tied to seniority and pay.

Even among 20+ year veterans, 49% use AI daily vs 24% who refuse — seniors adopt AI at the same rate as newcomers.

US developers average $167K — 94% more than Germany ($86K) — making geography the strongest predictor of raw salary.
